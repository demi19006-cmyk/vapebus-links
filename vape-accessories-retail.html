Accessories Support the Shift to Reusable Devices

Reusable vape products create a need for practical accessories: charging cables, spare glass, seals, cases, batteries where appropriate and cleaning items. Adult-facing retail content should explain what is genuinely useful without turning an accessory page into a vague add-on pitch.

After-sale support angle

A clear accessory page can show that a retailer understands long-term product care. It can also connect adults to safety basics such as using suitable chargers, checking device instructions and replacing worn parts before they cause avoidable problems.
